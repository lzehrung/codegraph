section: Fixed
---

MCP watchers keep watching through startup warmup and begin their full idle interval afterward. Lifecycle commands add early recovery of unexpired leases from provably dead holders in the same host, platform, Linux PID namespace, and Linux boot, including forcibly terminated discovery probes. Blocking or abandoned claims wait for freshly observed expiry instead of age-based retirement; recovery inherits the existing expiry procedure, including its late-stale-observer window, rather than providing a fully exclusive cross-process mutex.
